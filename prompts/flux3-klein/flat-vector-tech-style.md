# Flat-vector tech style (caller-side)

horus adds no style. This is the flat-vector look that the it-illustration
workflow used to add to every subject until 2026-11-09. A caller that wants this
look puts the opening sentences before its subject and the closing sentences
after it, in the subject that it sends to `generate_image`.

## Opening sentences

```text
A clean flat vector illustration for a modern tech explainer. It looks like a tidy technical diagram, with dark navy outlines of uniform medium weight and rounded corners, and solid flat pastel fills.
```

## Closing sentences

```text
The colour palette is dark navy for outlines, white, sky blue, lilac, mint green, sun yellow and coral, each with a light pastel tint for fills. Each object has one very soft flat shadow below it. The background is plain very pale blue with a faint sparse dotted grid and nothing else. Shapes are simple and friendly, with generous empty space. The whole scene is centred and fully inside the frame, with wide margins on all sides. Every surface is blank: screens and panels show only abstract coloured bars and blocks. The picture contains no text of any kind: no letters, no digits, no symbols and no logos.
```

## Result

The examples [message-queue](message-queue.md) and
